VUVU customer support page

A redesigned customer support page for a utility payment company.

Role
Chief Operating Officer
Timeline
6 months
Platform
Mobile web

The problem

The customer support page wasn’t working efficiently, which led to more calls and complaint messages.

What I did

I restructured the page around the most popular questions, so customers can find answers themselves before contacting support.

The result

The product is live and being downloaded from the web. Client feedback: “Fantastic.”

Yagapay app UI

A complete mobile app design for everyday bill, airtime, and utility payments.

Role
Chief Operating Officer
Timeline
6 months
Platform
Mobile app

The brief

One app where people can pay for airtime, data, and utilities, and manage their wallet in one place.

What I designed

19 screens covering onboarding, PIN login, payments, wallet, rewards, support, receipts, and empty and error states, so users always know what happened and what to do next.

The result

A full, consistent design system of screens that covers the main journeys and the edge cases.
